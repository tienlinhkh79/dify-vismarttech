# Golden Dragon Restaurant — Knowledge Base (US)

## About us
Golden Dragon is a family-owned Vietnamese restaurant in Houston, TX. We serve pho, banh mi, rice plates, vermicelli bowls, and bubble tea. Dine-in, takeout, and catering available.

## Dietary & allergies
- Gluten-sensitive: rice noodles and many rice plates are naturally gluten-free; ask staff about sauces
- Vegetarian/vegan options available — mark request when ordering
- Peanut used in some sauces — notify staff of allergies

## Ordering & delivery
- Takeout: call or order at counter
- Delivery partners: DoorDash, Uber Eats (menu prices may differ)
- Catering trays: 24-hour notice; min order $150

## FAQ
Q: Do you take reservations?
A: Yes — especially weekends. Leave your name, phone, date, time, and party size.

Q: Is the broth halal?
A: Our standard broth contains beef and chicken. Ask staff for vegetarian broth options.

Q: Do you have kids menu?
A: Yes — smaller pho and rice plates around $8.95.
